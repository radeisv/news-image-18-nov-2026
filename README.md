A dense newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026 in
one picture: 45 stories, 14 of them illustrated. A dark navy masthead
carries the date; below it, fourteen cards cover the day's biggest
stories — Iran's drones at the US base in Kuwait, Trump landing in
Shenzhen for the Xi–Putin talks, the Quad leaders meeting again in
Manila, 141 drones over Ukraine, the EU's proposed €45bn loan for
Ukraine, the Board of Peace warning the Gaza truce could collapse,
Voyager 1 reaching one light-day, the US losing its measles-free status,
Ebola passing 11,000 cases in DR Congo, the coalition strike on Sanaa,
the tanker hit in Hormuz, the OPCW's tear-gas finding, Germany's
power-tax cut, and GTA VI unlocking first in New Zealand. A numbers
strip, a dotted world map with 14 numbered pins, and a numbered "also
on Wednesday" list add 31 more, from Putin's peace-talk stance to
Cambridge's word of the year. A date-check box names the big stories
first reported on Tuesday 17 November that were deliberately left out.
Every story happened or was first reported on 18 November 2026;
disputed claims are attributed. All drawings are original.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This verifies `entry.png` (PNG, 1024×1024, under 1 MiB, complete IEND
chunk) and copies it to `out/entry.png`. In the grader it writes to
`/output/entry.png`.
